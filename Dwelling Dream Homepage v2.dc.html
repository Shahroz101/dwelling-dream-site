About the owner

Color should feel effortless.

Dwelling Dream began in my own half-painted living room, surrounded by forty paint chips and no idea which one was right. I spent years learning how light, undertone and sheen change a color once it is on the wall — and realized most people do not need more options. They need fewer, better ones.

Every palette here is built the way I build one for a client: nine colors tested together, in daylight and lamplight, in rooms that face every direction. Choose a mood, and the rest is already decided for you.

Shahroz— Founder, Dwelling Dream
